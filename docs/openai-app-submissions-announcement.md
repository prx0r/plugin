# App submissions announcement (OpenAI, Dec 2025 — distilled 2026-09-13)

Source: https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/
(Direct page fetch 403s; distilled from announcement excerpts + TechCrunch/Verge coverage.)

## What opened

- Developers can submit apps for review + publication via the OpenAI Developer
  Platform and track approval status there. First approved apps roll out
  gradually in the new year.
- New app directory inside ChatGPT (tools menu, chatgpt.com/apps); deep links
  can send users straight to an app page. Published apps appear in the
  universal Plugin directory shared by ChatGPT and Codex.
- Apps meeting quality/safety bars get listed; apps that resonate may be
  **featured or recommended by ChatGPT** — i.e. contextual surfacing is an
  explicit growth mechanic, not just search. This is the "App Store SEO for
  model routing" thesis from northstar.md, confirmed.

## Directly relevant to us

- **Physical-goods checkout: link out is allowed in this early phase.**
  "Developers can link out from their ChatGPT apps to their own websites or
  native apps to complete transactions for physical goods." So Pog Prints V1
  can quote/compare in-chat and hand off to our own checkout URL — no need to
  wait for Instant Checkout / Agentic Commerce integration.
- **Invocation is name + context based.** Users summon apps by name
  ("Spotify, make a playlist…") and ChatGPT proactively suggests apps mid-
  conversation (Zillow on home-buying talk). Our app description must own the
  utterances ("put this on a shirt", "my boiler's broken") — matches the
  northstar naming strategy.
- **Design bar:** tightly scoped, intuitive in chat, completes a real-world
  workflow that starts in conversation. Our 6-tool surface fits; 37-tool
  supplier mirrors would not.
- Resources published alongside: "what makes a great ChatGPT app" best
  practices, open-source example apps (openai-apps-sdk-examples), an
  open-sourced UI library for chat-native interfaces, quickstart.
- Pilot proof it works end to end: Booking.com/Expedia (booking + payment
  adjacent), Canva (create from conversation), Zillow (interactive map in
  chat). Our quote-comparison widget is the same pattern.
